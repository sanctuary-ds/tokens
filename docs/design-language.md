# Design language

Sanctuary treats an interface as a dark building lit through colored glass. Surfaces are stone and stay quiet; color is light and is spent sparingly, where it means something.

The design language now lives on the site, next to live examples:

- [Philosophy](https://sanctuary-ds.github.io/docs/philosophy): the one idea, the four materials, the three axes and what Sanctuary is not.
- Foundations: [color](https://sanctuary-ds.github.io/docs/foundations/color) (flavors, pigments, glass roles, glazing and windows), [light](https://sanctuary-ds.github.io/docs/foundations/light), [shape](https://sanctuary-ds.github.io/docs/foundations/shape), [type](https://sanctuary-ds.github.io/docs/foundations/type) and [motion](https://sanctuary-ds.github.io/docs/foundations/motion).
- [Accessibility](https://sanctuary-ds.github.io/docs/accessibility): the contrast guarantees, focus and reduced motion.

This repository keeps the [token reference](tokens.md) and the [architecture](architecture.md).
